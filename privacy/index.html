Privacy

Keep your context
under your control.

This website

The site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or browser storage. Fonts and scripts are served with the website. Your hosting provider may process ordinary access logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy. The current production host is GitHub Pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Setup inquiry drafts

The inquiry builder processes what you type only in the current browser page. It does not send the form to a server or persist it. “Open email app” hands a draft to your email application; “Copy brief” writes the brief to your clipboard when you choose it. You decide whether to send the email.

If you email behzad@airoweb.com, your email provider and the maintainer’s email provider handle the message. Behzad uses the details to respond and discuss your inquiry. Ask the same address for deletion of inquiry correspondence, subject to records that must be retained. Do not send private code, passwords or customer data.

The local toolkit

ADE’s CLI and hooks make no network requests. Files remain in the repository you choose. Any separate tools you use, including Claude Code, model providers and Git hosting, have their own policies. Using ADE does not change them.

Payments and donations

No checkout or donation destination is currently configured. If enabled later, hosted payment providers process payments under their own terms. A direct Bitcoin donation is recorded on a public blockchain; the website would only open your wallet and would not confirm payment.
